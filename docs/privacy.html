Privacy Policy

Gathered · Last updated: July 11, 2026

Gathered (“we,” “our,” or “us”) is a Bible reading and prayer app designed for small groups. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have when you use the Gathered mobile app and related services (collectively, the “Service”).

By using Gathered, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Who We Are

Gathered is operated by Jack Riley Garber. For privacy-related questions, contact: jrgarber4@gmail.com

2. Information We Collect

Account Information. When you create an account (via email and password, Google, or Sign in with Apple), we collect:

  • Email address (or Apple’s private relay email when you choose to hide your email)
  • Display name
  • Profile details you choose to provide (such as initials and avatar color)
  • Authentication identifiers from your sign-in provider

Group & User Content. When you use Gathered, we store content you create, including:

  • Group name and membership
  • Daily Bible reading entries (scripture reference and personal reflections)
  • Prayer requests, praise reports, reactions, and notes
  • Timestamps associated with your activity

Device & Notification Information. If you enable push notifications, we collect a device push token so we can send alerts when members of your group post prayers or respond with notes. You can disable notifications at any time in your device settings.

Technical Information. We use Firebase (Google) to operate authentication, data storage, and cloud functions. Firebase may process standard technical information such as IP address, device identifiers, and app usage data necessary to provide the Service securely.

We do not knowingly collect sensitive categories of personal information beyond what you voluntarily submit as prayer or reading content.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Authenticate you and keep your session secure
  • Store and sync your readings, prayers, and group activity
  • Enable sharing within your private group (2–5 members)
  • Send optional push notifications about group activity
  • Maintain, protect, and improve the Service
  • Respond to support requests

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your content to train AI models.

4. How Information Is Shared

Within Your Group. Content you post—readings, prayers, reactions, and notes—is visible to other members of your group.

Service Providers. We use trusted third-party providers to operate Gathered, including Google Firebase (authentication, database, cloud functions, and push notification delivery). These providers process data on our behalf only to deliver the Service.

Sign-In Providers. If you choose Google or Apple sign-in, those providers authenticate you according to their own privacy policies. We receive only the information needed to create and maintain your Gathered account.

Legal Requirements. We may disclose information if required by law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of users and the Service.

We do not share your information with advertisers.

5. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service. If you delete your account or request deletion, we will delete or anonymize your personal information within a reasonable period, except where retention is required by law or for legitimate security purposes.

6. Security

We use industry-standard safeguards through Firebase, including encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and access controls. Only authenticated members of a group can access that group’s readings and prayers.

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. We encourage you to use a strong, unique password and keep your device secure.

7. Your Choices & Rights

You may:

  • Update your profile in the app’s Settings
  • Disable push notifications in iOS Settings
  • Request account deletion by contacting us at jrgarber4@gmail.com

Depending on your location, you may have additional rights to access, correct, or delete your personal data. We will honor valid requests in accordance with applicable law.

8. Children’s Privacy

Gathered is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

9. International Users

Your information may be processed and stored in the United States or other countries where our service providers operate. By using Gathered, you consent to this transfer.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the revised policy with a new “Last updated” date. Continued use of Gathered after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
